# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.

## The detail

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Unacceptable: harassment, personal attacks, publishing anyone's private information,
and sustained disruption of discussion.

Expected: welcoming language, respect for differing viewpoints, graceful acceptance
of constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for people using this.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Wosmos). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may
edit, remove, or reject contributions that breach this, and may ban repeat offenders.
